Abstract

We describe GrokDev, an always-on digital entity that observes Solana memecoin culture, scores narratives, and refuses to mint unless a human authority names a ticker in plain text. The system is conceptually adjacent to BWA (a high-throughput headline factory) but is not BWA: selectivity is the brand. This paper records the public machine — wallet prefix grok, sibling occupancy by GrokBot / GROKIUS, the ticker armory, the observe loop, the desk and stream kit, and the honest engine state. No transaction is claimed. Genesis remains unpublished (0 mint).

Keywords — compressed attention; autonomous deployer; pump.fun; GrokBot; GROKIUS; paper trading of culture; gated launch

1. Introduction

A memecoin is not an asset class so much as a compression of attention: culture + timing + distribution + identity + speculation, forced into a ticker.1 Most autonomous deployers optimize for speed. GrokDev optimizes for a public track record. The operating inequality is simple: we would rather miss a ten-second race than ship garbage.

The closest public reference is BWA (pump handle turbomantasacre), wallet bwamJzztZsepfkteWRChggmXuiiCQvpLqPietdNfSXa. That system is a firehose: name from post, empty bio, high fire-rate. GrokDev is the filter. Ticker collision on the string “BWA” is already observed; we treat BWA as a style citation, not a coin to copy.

3. The loop

There is no final task. The life of the machine is a cycle. Urgent tape may interrupt lower work. A trigger is not a deploy. A deploy is a human sentence.

OBSERVE → RESEARCH → THINK → THESIS → ACT → PUBLISH → LEARN
(1)

Observe fires on weekdays at 09 / 13 / 17 / 21, America/Santiago. If nothing is grade A or S, the machine stays quiet.

4. Scoring

Signals are not equal. We ask who posted, how fast it is spreading, who is amplifying, whether a meme can be remixed, whether a canonical token already exists, and whether attention is accelerating or decaying. Grades used for proposals (never auto-fire):

  • S — global meme, platform event, or undeniable proper noun
  • A — independent accounts arriving at the same joke
  • B — funny, thin; stock the ticker, do not ask to launch
  • F — slop, ragebait, stolen character; refuse

7. Authorization

Autonomy does not invent permissions. Only provisioned wallets, accounts, and APIs are used. Irreversible actions wait for the exact phrase launch <TICKER> from CABAL. No rugs, no fake KOL, no stolen IP, no fabricated transactions. Private keys are never published, streamed, or written into this paper.
